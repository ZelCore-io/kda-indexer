KDA Indexer vs Stock

An independent audit of the G4 replay of real production requests: what was compared, how it was compared, and every difference found between ours and stock (the Kadena GraphQL indexer, chainweb-data and kda-txs). Verdict

Detecting differences is sound. Every response is compared as full JSON, fail-closed. Comparing by position can over-report differences but can't hide one.

Explaining and closing them is not sound yet. Most remaining differences were closed by scripts that live only on the test server and were never reviewed. A few labels contradict the harness's own rules. Two kinds of check were never done: pages after the first, and sets inside paged block lists. At the audit, 24 of the 43 kinds weren't in the public docs; the docs now cover all of them except the 2 not yet classified (#38, #39).

First page only, counts only: what the comparison actually covers

Each real request was replayed exactly as the client sent it, and both full answers were compared. So the comparison covers exactly the page the client asked for, and nothing beyond it.

Planned fixes for these gaps

  • Page-walk check: for a sample of every paged query shape, walk each side to its last page with its own cursors. Compare the combined rows as sets, plus totalCount, and check rows found on only one side against the node.
  • Paged block transactions: walk all 515 blocks to the end on both sides and check them against the node payload. Fix the doc line.
  • Harness: set-based compare for balance and /v1/txs, an "incomplete" column in every table, and the closure scripts moved into the repo and reviewed.
  • Live replay on the release build: isCoinbase (5,193), token price (49) and a 2,000-case sample, at no more than 4 parallel requests to production.
  • Ours-only families: check nonFungibleAccount, /txs/recent and /txs/search against the node.

Updates

  • 25 Nov: PR #34 merged. It fixes the bridge-token balance marking (#43), documents the lago exclusion (#41), and documents that balance(...) stays fungible-only (#42). A memory problem the review found is fixed too: a crafted transaction of 0.5 MB used to cost 968 MB, and now costs 0.7 MB. Deployment and the re-walk are in progress.
  • 25 Nov: Security re-check of the 10 open audit items. 2 were real but low impact (R-01, F-52) and are fixed in PR #35, merged together with an F-58 hardening. The other 8 don't apply in our setup, because they need our own trusted node to misbehave or can't be reached on our install.
